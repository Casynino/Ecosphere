We're proud that our collaborator, the Executive Director of the Pakistan Research Center for a Community with Shared Future, Mr. Khalid Taimur Akram, was a distinguished speaker at the Phewa Dialogue 2026 in Chengdu, China (March 26-27).

Theme: "South Asian Development under Climate Change and Governance"

## Key highlights from Mr. Akram's talk

- Climate change is a regional challenge, not just a national issue
- Promote cross-border climate-resilient infrastructure
- Strengthen renewable energy and sustainable agriculture
- Enhance regional trade and policy harmonization
- Foster multi-stakeholder innovation: academia, business, civil society and youth

Message: South Asia's shared challenges can become collective opportunities, and global cooperation is essential. By working together regionally and internationally, we can achieve green growth, economic resilience, and sustainable prosperity for all.

We both collaborate in turning our vision into action, driving innovation, sustainability, and collaboration across borders.
