Norga Komba, in her capacity as an Assistant Lecturer in Law at Jordan University College, had the distinct honor of serving as a delegate and speaker at the Forum on China-Africa Cooperation (FOCAC) Legal Forum 2025, held in Changsha, China, on 10–11 July 2025.

The forum brought together legal and law enforcement professionals from China and 26 African countries to strengthen cooperation on legal affairs and advance dialogue on economic and trade relations between China and Africa.

Norga was part of a distinguished Tanzanian delegation that included Dustan Shimbo (Director of Legal Services, Vice President's Office), Prof. William Anangisye (Vice Chancellor of the University of Dar es Salaam), Dr. Laurean Mussa (Dean of Law), and Said Musa (Legal Affairs Representative, Ministry of Education, Zanzibar).

This collective effort successfully showcased Tanzania's commitment to deepening legal cooperation in economy and trade, reinforcing the shared vision for a robust China-Africa community with a shared future. A truly impactful representation for our country and institution.
