We are proud to announce that our Compliance and Regulatory Director, Adv. Violet William Mushi, successfully completed the "Seminar on Small and Medium-Sized Enterprise Owners' Successful Practices in Silk Road E-Commerce for Developing Countries" held in Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China, in September 2025.

The program was sponsored by the Ministry of Commerce of China and focused on strengthening e-commerce capacity and international trade collaboration among developing countries.
